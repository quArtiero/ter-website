Formula Hybrid+Electric · Tufts University

We design, build and race an electric formula car.

Student-run at Tufts since 2008. Every spring we take a car we built in the Bray Labs garage to New Hampshire Motor Speedway — and every fall we start again. Any major, no experience needed.

What we do

Everyone meets on Sundays at noon in the garage. During the week, each project team meets once to design, build and test its piece of the car.

Mechanical

Chassis, suspension, brakes, drivetrain, cooling and aero, seat, accumulator enclosure and cart. CAD in Onshape and FEA first, then metalwork, welding, composites and 3D printing in Bray Labs.

  • Chassis
  • Suspension
  • Brakes
  • Drivetrain
  • Cooling & Aero
  • Seat
  • Accumulator

Electrical

Accumulator and high voltage, a custom battery management system, the low-voltage harness and safety systems, sensors and data. Soldering, PCB design and embedded code — we teach circuits from zero.

  • Low Voltage
  • Accumulator / HV
  • BMS
  • Sensors

Business Operations

Sponsorships and outreach, budget and purchasing, social media and events, logistics and compliance. The budgets, partners and plans that get the car — and the team — to New Hampshire.

  • Sponsorships & Outreach
  • Budget & Purchasing
  • Social Media & Events
  • Logistics & Compliance

The build

TER27: a new car from scratch

Our first new chassis since 2019 — a two-year design-and-build program aimed at the 2027 competition. New accumulator, custom BMS, redesigned suspension and aero, all designed and manufactured in-house.

Latest from the garage · October 2026

  • Low-voltage wiring harness design is nearing completion; safety systems are heading to fabrication.
  • High-voltage designs are on the bench for testing.
  • Pedal box: topology study on the pedal shape, FEA on the box itself.
  • Hub spacers going into manufacturing; rotor research under way.
