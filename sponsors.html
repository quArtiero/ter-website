Sponsorship · 2026–27 season

Put your logo on a race car.

Your sponsorship — monetary, materials, services or mentorship — lets about 90 Tufts students design, build and race an electric formula car in the Formula Hybrid+Electric competition. We work with partners from small businesses to multinationals, and we keep the relationship going well past race day.

Why support us

In sponsoring TER, you will

01

Connect with experienced engineering students

Sponsor days, vehicle demonstrations and access to our resume book — students who have worked to a real design schedule under real constraints.

02

Promote your organization to Tufts and beyond

Logo placement on the car, this website and team apparel, plus acknowledgement across our social media and at the competition in New Hampshire.

03

Show your commitment to engineering education

We work alongside Tufts University to bring hands-on STEM engagement to the surrounding community — an impact that lasts beyond the team itself.

Ways to support

Not just a check

  • Monetary gifts

    Fund the parts we cannot make ourselves — motor controller, battery cells, tires — and the trip to New Hampshire.

  • Materials, parts & software

    Composites and vacuum-bagging supplies, electronics, machine time, CAD and simulation licenses. Most of our partners support us this way.

  • Services, food & mentorship

    Truck rental for competition week, meals for build days, design reviews with your engineers.
